Wheels

Forged aero wheel.

Five blades of diamond-cut aluminium, each backed with warm titanium. Shaped to pull air out of the arch and cool the rotors behind.

  • Warm titanium

A single blade of red.

One continuous optical emitter spans the tail, set under a smoked lens. Below it, the diffuser frames the thruster ports.
